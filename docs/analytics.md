# Analytics methodology

The analytics layer aggregates actual review-level sentiment and rating data into dashboard metrics.

## Metrics

- total reviews
- positive / neutral / negative counts
- percentage breakdowns
- average rating
- rating distribution
- sentiment trend over time
- word frequency for top themes

## Product intelligence score

The system derives a simple explainable score from the weighted review mix and average rating, without relying on opaque hidden rules.

The scoring combines:

- rating health
- sentiment balance
- review volume
- consistency of positive vs negative feedback
